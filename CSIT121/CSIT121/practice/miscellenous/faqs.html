Frequently Asked Questions

Do you offer plant care advice after purchase?
Yes. Our team is happy to answer questions any time, whether you bought your plant from us last week or last year.
Can I return a plant if it doesn't survive?
We offer a 30 day guarantee on all plants. Bring in the plant along with your receipt and we'll help make it right.
Do you deliver?
Yes, we offer local delivery within a 15 mile radius. Delivery fees vary based on distance and order size.
What's the difference between organic and synthetic fertilizers?
Organic fertilizers are made from natural materials and release nutrients slowly over time. Synthetic fertilizers are manufactured to provide a precise, fast-acting nutrient boost. Our staff can help you decide which is right for your plants.
I'm new to plant care. Where should I start?
Check out our Plant Care and Plant Inspiration sections for tips, or stop by the store for a one-on-one consultation with our staff.
Do you sell gift cards?
Yes, gift cards are available in store in any denomination.
How do I know which fertilizer is right for my plant?
It depends on the plant type, its growth stage, and any specific deficiencies it may have. Our Specialized Fertilizers section covers targeted solutions, or you can ask our staff for a recommendation.
Can I bring in a plant for identification?
Absolutely. Bring in a photo or a cutting and our staff will help you identify it and provide care tips.
