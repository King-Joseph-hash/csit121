GIF Format Example

About This Image

This is an animated GIF with elements that continuously move and repeat. It is extracted from the popular animated cartoon, Regular Show. The GIF itself shows Rigby, one of the main characters from the show, studying, which is our theme.

Why GIF Format?

GIF works perfectly for this because the GIF format supports animation and can repeat automatically, making it perfect for looping animated GIFs. Animated GIFs also do not require a video player, which means they can show movement directly within a webpage.
