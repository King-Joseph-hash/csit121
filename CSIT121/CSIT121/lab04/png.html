PNG Format Example

About This Image

This is a PNG image that shows two dark-colored books stacked on top of each other. The image only uses two colors: black for the outer cover of the books and white for the pages (but the white parts are actually transparent and will match the background).

Why PNG Format?

PNGs support transparent backgrounds which is useful since the books have a transparent background. PNGs are good for images with simple shapes and limited colors, such as our black-and-white books.
