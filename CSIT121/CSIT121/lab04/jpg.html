JPG Format Example

About This Image

This photograph shows a picture of a notebook with a bunch of text in it. The text consists of answers to theory questions found in a textbook. The creator of the notes created them for an assignment, and all the answers were derived from information found in the “Web Standards” textbook.

Why JPG Format?

JPG works perfectly for this because JPG compresses the image, making the file smaller and easier to store or upload. JPG is also widely supported on every computer, phone, web-browser, etc., meaning the image has little chance of failing to load.
